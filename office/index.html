◊ Seed Office

Plant your seed

Your office grows here, on this device, from one signed seed. Every block is checked against the seed's pins before it runs. Nothing leaves this device unless you send it.

  1. It is the only key to your office: write it down and keep it safe. Nobody else holds it, so nobody else can recover it. At least 16 characters.

Or regrow your office

Lost a machine, or moving to a new one? Plant the seed again and open your vault with your phrase: everything comes back, byte for byte. This device gets a new key of its own, and your behaviour watch starts fresh in observe-only, so it can never lock you out.

    Files

    Bring files in from the old world: PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), CSV, email (EML), calendars (ICS), contacts (VCF), images and zip archives. Each file's bytes are checked before anything reads them; it is then read in a sandbox with no network, and its original is kept unchanged. A file that fails is kept out, and you are told why. Office macros are always refused.

    Your vault
    
      
    One encrypted file holds everything this office keeps. Only your phrase opens it: the vault holds no key, and nobody else holds your phrase, so nobody else can open it, or recover it for you. Keep a copy somewhere safe, off this machine.
    
      
      
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Your devices
    
      
    One identity, many devices. Each device has a key of its own; your phrase signs the list of your devices, and every change that passes between them is a signed packet checked before it is read. Pairing uses codes you copy from one device to the other: no server, and no address on the internet is ever asked for.
